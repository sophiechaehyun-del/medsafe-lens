Foods, drinks and supplements

Not medical advice. MedSafe Lens uses AI to read images and summarize general drug information. It can misread labels, may be incomplete or out of date, and does not know your health conditions, other medicines or allergies. Label excerpts come from public U.S. openFDA data and may not match products sold elsewhere. The AI writes summaries from the ingredient name and FDA text; photos are sent to the AI service only when you choose “Check the photo with AI” or add label photos to an interaction check. Never start, stop or change a medicine because of what you see here; speak with a pharmacist or prescriber.
